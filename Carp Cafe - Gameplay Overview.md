# Carp Café — Gameplay Overview

## Game Concept

Carp Café is a relaxing koi-pond simulation game inspired by the experience of visiting a real carp café in Asia.

The game is centered around a simple, satisfying loop: players care for koi fish, feed them, help them grow, build their collection, and gradually customize and improve their pond.

The experience is designed to feel calm and approachable while still giving players clear progression goals, collectibles, upgrades, and friendly global competition.

---

## Core Gameplay Loop

The main gameplay loop revolves around interacting with the fish and growing the player’s pond.

Players:

1. Feed the koi
2. Help the fish grow
3. Earn value from raising fish
4. Sell selected fish
5. Use the rewards to acquire new fish and pond scenery
6. Expand and improve their collection
7. Progress through the game and compete on the global leaderboard

The player continually cycles through feeding, growing, collecting, selling, and upgrading.

---

## Feeding the Fish

Feeding is one of the main interactive elements of the game.

Players drop food into the pond, attracting nearby koi. The fish swim toward the food and gather around it to eat.

This creates one of the game’s main visual moments, with the fish moving toward the player and their faces becoming more visible as they approach the food.

Feeding contributes to the growth and progression of the fish, encouraging players to regularly return to their pond and interact with them.

---

## Fish Growth

Fish begin at a smaller size and gradually grow as the player continues caring for and feeding them.

Growth gives the player a visible sense of progression. A fish that begins as a small koi can eventually become significantly larger and more valuable.

Different fish can therefore represent different stages of the player’s progress.

The player’s goal is not only to collect fish, but also to develop them over time.

---

## Selling Fish

Once fish have grown, players can choose to sell them.

Selling fish provides resources that can then be reinvested into the pond.

This creates an important strategic choice:

- Keep a fish as part of the collection.
- Continue growing it.
- Or sell it to fund new purchases and upgrades.

The system creates a continuous economy in which the fish themselves are both collectibles and part of the player’s progression.

---

## Collecting New Koi

Players can acquire different varieties of koi throughout the game.

Each new fish adds more visual variety to the pond and gives players another fish to raise.

Collecting different koi becomes a long-term objective, encouraging players to discover and obtain more varieties rather than simply raising the same type of fish repeatedly.

As the collection expands, the player’s pond becomes increasingly unique.

---

## Pond Customization

Players can also spend their earned resources on decorative scenery for the pond.

These items allow players to personalize the environment and create different versions of their own koi pond.

Scenery may include elements such as:

- Plants
- Rocks
- Water features
- Pond decorations
- Environmental objects
- Different visual themes

Customization gives progression a visual reward. As players advance, their pond can become more detailed and personalized.

---

## Progression

Progression is built around gradually increasing the value and variety of the player’s pond.

As players continue playing, they can:

- Raise larger fish
- Unlock or purchase new koi
- Earn more resources
- Acquire more scenery
- Expand their collection
- Improve the overall appearance and value of their pond

The game is designed so that even short interactions contribute toward longer-term progression.

---

## Player Economy

The game’s economy connects the major gameplay systems together.

Feed fish → Grow fish → Sell fish → Earn resources → Purchase new fish or scenery → Grow the pond

This creates a simple system that is easy to understand while still giving the player meaningful decisions about where to spend their resources.

Players can choose whether to focus primarily on collecting fish, growing valuable koi, decorating their pond, or progressing competitively.

---

## Global Leaderboard

Carp Café also includes a global leaderboard.

The leaderboard gives players a way to compare their progress with other players around the world.

This adds a competitive layer to what is otherwise a relaxing single-player experience.

Players can work toward improving their position by progressing further, growing their pond, and increasing their overall in-game performance.

The leaderboard gives highly engaged players an additional long-term objective without changing the calm nature of the core gameplay.

---

## Overall Player Experience

Carp Café combines the relaxing feeling of watching and feeding koi with light progression and collection mechanics.

The experience is intended to be:

**Relaxing** — watching fish swim and feeding them is the central experience.

**Satisfying** — fish visibly grow as the player cares for them.

**Collectible** — players can discover and acquire different koi varieties.

**Customizable** — players can build their own version of the pond.

**Progressive** — feeding, growing, selling, and purchasing continuously move the player forward.

**Competitive when desired** — the global leaderboard gives players an optional reason to compare their progress with others.

At its core, Carp Café transforms the simple experience of feeding koi into a small virtual ecosystem where players can care for fish, build a collection, personalize their environment, and gradually create their ideal koi pond.
